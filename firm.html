What we do

Three services, usually taken together. No hourly surprises — you know your monthly number before we start.

Tax preparation & filing

Individual 1040 and Schedule C, 1065 partnerships, 1120-S and 1120 corporate returns — prepared, reviewed and e-filed, with extensions handled when you need the time.

  • Rental property (Schedule E) and crypto in scope
  • Quarterly estimated payments calculated for you
  • Prior-year catch-up and amended returns

Monthly bookkeeping

QuickBooks Online done properly: bank and card reconciliation, categorisation, month-end close — and catch-up work if you are months or years behind.

  • Clean books that make tax season boring
  • Monthly close you can actually read
  • Receipt and document follow-up handled for you

Payroll

Payroll run on schedule, 941 filed quarterly, 940 annually, W-2s and 1099-NECs out by the end of January — including reasonable-compensation payroll for S-corp owners.

  • Employees and subcontractors both covered
  • New-hire and year-end reporting included
  • One number, no per-filing add-ons
